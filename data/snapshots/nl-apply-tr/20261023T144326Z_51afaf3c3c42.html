Applying for a Schengen visa for the Netherlands in Türkiye

If you will be visiting the Netherlands for a maximum of 90 days – for a holiday, to do business or to see family, for example – you can apply for a Schengen visa. Follow the steps below to apply in Türkiye.

Step 1: Check before applying

Before starting your application, check whether you need a visa for the Netherlands and, if so, where you can apply for it.

Not everyone needs a visa to visit the Netherlands. If you’re unsure what applies to you, check whether you need a visa to visit the Netherlands.

Don’t need a visa?

If you don’t need a visa for the Netherlands, see what documents you do need to travel to the Netherlands.

Check what you need to travel to the Netherlands

Do you provide services?

You do not need a visa if you meet the following 3 conditions:

  1. You have Turkish nationality.
  2. You are coming to the Netherlands for a short period (maximum 3 months out of 6 months) to provide services.
  3. You are employed by an employer in Türkiye, or you are self-employed.

You must be able to prove at the Dutch border that you meet the conditions. For example, with documents such as an employment contract or an extract from a Chamber of Commerce.

It is also strongly recommended that a statement free of charge be requested from the Dutch embassy in Ankara or the consulate-general in Istanbul. Send an email to the embassy in Ankara (ank-ca@minbuza.nl) or the consulate-general in Istanbul (ist-ca@minbuza.nl). Please note that the statement does not guarantee that you will enter the Netherlands.

To apply for a Schengen visa in Türkiye go to the VFS Global application centre in Ankara, Antalya, Bodrum, Bursa, Edirne, Gaziantep, Istanbul or Izmir. You can submit an application there if you live in one of the following places:

  • Türkiye
  • Iraq
  • Syria

If you live somewhere else, go to the page Applying for a Schengen visa and select the country where you live to find out where to submit your application. If you still want to submit your application in Türkiye, first check if that’s possible.

Check whether you can apply for a visa outside your country of residence

If you want to visit another Schengen country besides the Netherlands, check whether you should apply for a visa for the Netherlands or the other country.

Step 2: Gather the documents you need

You need several documents to apply for a Schengen visa. Always submit all the documents required. Your application can be rejected if you do not do this.

Find out which documents you need to gather for your application.

You will need a completed and signed Schengen visa application form. Print this form and submit it along with the other documents.

Fill out the Schengen visa application form online

Complete your own application form online. Only then will you receive a form with a unique code. Forms without this code may cause issues while processing your application. For that reason, do not use someone else’s form.

You will need all the documents on your checklist. To find out which documents you need, select the checklist that corresponds to your purpose of travel. If you have more than one purpose of travel, select the checklist that corresponds to your primary purpose of travel.

Step 3: Make an appointment

Once you have all the documents you need for your Schengen visa application, make an appointment at VFS Global.

You can apply for your visa up to 6 months before your trip. If you are a seafarer, you can apply for a visa up to 9 months in advance. Apply for your visa at least 15 calendar days before the date on which you will be travelling.

Make an appointment online at VFS Global in Türkiye

  • VFS Global is the only place in Türkiye where you can apply for a visa for the Netherlands.
  • Avoid unnecessary costs and book your appointment yourself. Do not make an appointment through an intermediary.
  • If you are applying for visas for multiple people, make a separate appointment for each person.

You can authorise someone else to submit your visa application on your behalf if you meet all of the following conditions:

  • You are 12 years or older.
  • You applied in person for a Schengen, airport transit or entry visa within the past 59 months.
  • During that application, your fingerprints were taken.

The 59 months are counted back from the date your new application is submitted.

Documents they need to bring

The person submitting your application must bring:

  • a letter of authorisation, signed by you and by them
  • their travel document (for example, a passport) and a copy thereof
  • all your documents on the checklist from Step 2

Please note

If your fingerprints were taken in the past 59 months, you do not need to do this again. Fingerprints stored in the Visa Information System (VIS) can be copied by the Dutch Ministry. The external service provider cannot access this system to check your fingerprints.

In case your fingerprints cannot be copied by the Ministry, you will be asked to come back to provide your fingerprints at the application location. To avoid this, you can always have your fingerprints taken during your visa application. Even if you already did so in the past 59 months.

To change or cancel your appointment, contact VFS Global in Türkiye.

Step 4: Go to your appointment

Go to your appointment at VFS Global, and bring all the documents on your checklist from step 2 with you, plus the checklist itself.

See the address of VFS Global in Türkiye

At your appointment you:

  • Submit the documents that are on your checklist, together with your passport.
  • Provide your fingerprints. Some exceptions apply, read about them below.
  • Answer questions about your trip.
  • Pay the visa fees.
  • Indicate whether you want to collect your passport (with visa affixed) or have it sent to you.
  • Receive a reference number to track the status of your application.

Exceptions to fingerprint requirement

In the following situations you are not required to provide fingerprints:

  • You are under the age of 12. Please note: Children under the age of 12 must still be present at the appointment.
  • You are physically unable to provide fingerprints and have a medical document to show this.
  • You have applied for a Schengen visa or an entry visa in the past 59 months and your fingerprints were taken for that application.
  • You are travelling in your capacity as head of state, government leader, or member of a national government or official delegation (or as the spouse of any of these) in the context of an official visit. And you were invited by the Dutch government or an international organisation.
  • You are travelling in your capacity as member of the royal family in the context of an official visit. And you were invited by the Dutch government or an international organisation.

See the overview of consular fees in Türkiye to find out how much a visa costs and what forms of payment are accepted. In some situations you are not required to pay.

Find out in which situations you do not need to pay the visa fee

You will always need to pay service costs to VFS Global.

Find out more about processing times for a visa application

When you submit your application you’ll receive a reference number from VFS Global that you can use to track the status of your application online.

Step 5: Collect your passport or have it sent to you

You will receive an email once your application has been processed. The email won’t say whether your application was successful. You can collect your passport or have it sent to you. If your application was approved, the visa will be affixed to a page in your passport.

If you choose to have your passport sent to you, you’ll discuss at your appointment how to arrange this.

If you choose to collect your passport, you’ll discuss at your appointment when you can collect it. You can also have someone else collect your passport for you.

If you collect your passport yourself, bring the following with you:

  • a valid form of ID
  • the proof of payment provided by VFS Global

If someone else collects your passport for you, they must bring the following with them:

  • a valid form of ID
  • a signed letter from you authorising them to collect your passport
  • the proof of payment provided by VFS Global

Check whether the information on your visa is correct. Your name and the visa’s validity period, for example.

Find out more about the information on your visa

If any of the information is incorrect or you can’t use the visa, for example because the period of validity is later than the dates of the plane tickets in your application, contact the Dutch embassy in Ankara as soon as possible.

If there’s a mistake on your visa, the airline may not let you board your flight or you may be refused entry into the Schengen area.

Email the Dutch embassy

If your application wasn’t approved, you’ll receive a letter stating why. If you disagree with this decision, you have several options.

Find out what you can do if your visa application was refused

Step 6: Prepare for your trip

With your Schengen visa you can travel to the Netherlands or another Schengen country. The visa allows you to stay in the Schengen area for a maximum of 90 days in any 180-day period. Your visa will be valid for a specific period, which may also be shorter than 90 days.

You must show the visa upon departure and arrival. Border control may also ask you for other documents. Find out what other documents you need in the checklist for travelling to the Netherlands.

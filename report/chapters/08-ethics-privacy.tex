\section{Use of AI assistants}\label{sec:ai-use}
An AI assistant (Claude, Anthropic) was used during development to draft code, tests,
documentation and parts of this report. Its output was not accepted as given: the author analysed
every proposal in detail and tested all of it, with the automated tests run locally before every
pull request and the application tried by hand in a browser and on a phone, and changed or
rejected what did not fit. The scope, the requirements and every design decision, recorded as
ADRs, are the author's own, and the author is responsible for the whole content.

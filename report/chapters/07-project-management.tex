\chapter{Project Management}\label{ch:management}

\section{Methodology}
The project is developed by one person in weekly increments. Every piece of work starts as an
issue with a completion criterion, is built on its own branch and reaches \texttt{main} through a
pull request that passes the automated checks (Section~\ref{sec:tools}). Decisions that would be
expensive to reverse, or that depart from the proposal, are recorded as architecture decision
records (ADRs) in \texttt{docs/decisions/}; eight exist by 23 November 2026. A weekly note in
\texttt{docs/weekly/} records what was done, what was demonstrated, the problems met, the limits
of the work and the questions for the advisor.

The advisor is met on Fridays. Meetings took place on 23 October and 20 November 2026; the three
in between did not, and the weekly notes and questions were sent by e-mail instead. So that the
work would not wait for the answers, the open questions were decided as proposed and recorded in
ADR 0006 together with the cost of reversing each; at the meeting of 20 November the advisor kept
all of them and changed one plan, the timing of the model (Section~\ref{sec:ai-design}).
